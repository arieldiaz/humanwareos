# Compound

Use after a review or repeated observation reveals something likely to change future judgment.

1. Separate durable facts and preferences from temporary conditions, trivia, and proposed procedures. Route procedures through [Rule promotion](docs/agent-context-hierarchy.md#rule-promotion) before curating factual context.
2. Search the current projection for the same lesson. Strengthen or replace it rather than adding a near-duplicate.
3. Append evidence with provenance, then update the smallest relevant projection.
4. Remove stale projection text when the new fact supersedes it.

Done when future context is more useful and no larger than necessary.
