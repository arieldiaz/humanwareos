# Challenge

Use when the human requests it or when a genuinely consequential decision would benefit from an independent opposing view. It is optional for ordinary work.

Give a fresh reviewer only the decision or deliverable and the relevant constraints. Prefer a different capable model when available. Return no more than five lines:

1. the strongest objection;
2. one important point the owner may have missed;
3. a verdict: proceed, amend, or ask the human.

The owner answers the objection once. Persistent disagreement goes to the human without forced consensus. The challenger advises; it does not create a veto or a new lifecycle state.
