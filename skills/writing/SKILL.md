# Writing

## Overview

Neutral Life OS writing capability. Customize it per person via an instance
overlay (see the private-instance pattern used by `video-editor-social`).

A writing skill has two modes that share one goal: **fidelity of thought.**

- **Writer** — create. Get the idea down, including the path that led to it.
- **Editor** — improve. Sharpen clarity without changing the thought or erasing
  how it was reached.

They are one skill because they are one spectrum. A separate `librarian` skill
connects ideas across time; this skill works within a single piece.

## The core principle

The unit of optimization is not writing quality. It is fidelity of thought.

The best writing is not what sounds best — it is what most accurately captures
how a person actually arrived at an idea. An observation leads to a tangent, the
tangent reveals a contradiction, the contradiction becomes an insight. Editing
away everything except the insight makes the piece cleaner and less true. What
made the idea interesting was the path, not the conclusion.

Leave behind an accurate map of a mind exploring reality.

## Writer mode

1. Capture the idea in the author's own voice; do not pre-polish.
2. Preserve the journey — the tangents, false starts, and the moment the idea
   turned. These are signal, not noise.
3. Prefer the author's phrasing over "better" phrasing when the two conflict.
4. Surface unresolved questions rather than papering over them.

## Editor mode

1. Improve clarity without changing the thought.
2. Cut redundancy and hedging; keep the reasoning steps that show the path.
3. Never trade fidelity for smoothness. If a cleaner sentence hides how the
   author got there, keep the truer one.
4. Flag — do not silently remove — anything that changes meaning.

## Boundaries

1. **Voice belongs to the author.** This skill assists; it does not overwrite.
2. **Do not homogenize.** Resist flattening idiosyncratic thinking into generic
   prose.
3. **Distribution is a separate concern.** Turning an essay into posts/threads
   belongs in a distribution skill, not here.
4. **Instances customize via overlay.** Keep this version neutral; author-specific
   themes, references, and taste live in the private-instance copy.
