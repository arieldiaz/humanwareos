# Act

Use when the requested outcome is clear enough to execute.

1. Complete one useful slice before opening another.
2. Verify that slice against its observable completion condition at the level its risk deserves.
3. Leave the durable trail appropriate to the work: saved artifact, external readback, test result, or commit.
4. If reality invalidates the plan, stop forcing it and reframe the affected decision.

Done when the requested outcome is met with evidence, or when a verified blocker remains after safe in-scope alternatives are exhausted.
